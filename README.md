# Office Wiz

AI Workplace Productivity Assistant — Short Development Prompt

Build a modern, responsive AI-powered web application that integrates three tools into one platform: Smart Email Generator, Meeting Notes Summarizer, and AI Research Assistant.

Core Features

Smart Email Generator

Input: Email purpose, recipient, subject, and preferred tone.

Output: Professional email drafts with options to edit, copy, save, and regenerate.

Meeting Notes Summarizer

Input: Meeting notes, transcripts, date, and attendees.

Output: Meeting summaries, key points, decisions, action items, responsibilities, and deadlines.

AI Research Assistant

Input: Research topic, questions, objectives, or documents.

Output: Research reports, key findings, insights, recommendations, and reliable citations when available.

Dashboard and Design

Integrate all three tools into one dashboard with simple navigation.

Include saved results, activity history, and quick-access tool cards.

Use a clean white background, navy text, and blue accents.

Ensure the application works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Technical Requirements

Build with React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, or a suitable supported stack.

Integrate AI services through a secure backend.

Store saved results securely and protect confidential information.

Include loading indicators, error handling, and copy, edit, save, and download options.

Final Goal

Create one functional AI workplace productivity application that saves time, improves communication, organizes meeting information, and makes research easier through a single platform.
